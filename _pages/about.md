I am a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/) and [Mila - Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/en), supervised by Prof. [David Rolnick](https://davidrolnick.com/). My research focuses on machine learning for biodiversity monitoring, including using machine learning to accelerate species discovery and assess restoration success. 

I obtained my B.Sc. from McGill in the Joint Honours Mathematics and Computer Science program, with a minor in Environment. During my undergrad, I worked on projects related to calcium diffusion in astrocytes, land cover type classification, topic modeling, and machine learning storage.
